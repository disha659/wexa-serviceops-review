    Questions

    Asked directly

    Does this replace our ticket tool?+

    No. Your ticket tool stays the system of record. Wexa raises, reads and updates tickets in it, for example ServiceNow, Jira or Zendesk.

    Do we need developers to build automations?+

    No. Describe the task in plain English, or start from a resolved ticket. Every automation is tested and approved before it goes live.

    What can the AI do without approval?+

    Only what your policies allow. Sensitive actions, such as granting access or creating accounts, pause for a named approver.

    Where does our data live?+

    In Wexa cloud, or in your own VPC, on-premises or air-gapped. Personal data is masked before any AI model sees it.
